# Prompt for Claude Fable 5 — FSK Codehouse Go-to-Market & Pricing

Paste this into a Claude Fable 5 session that has web search / browsing tools enabled. It's written so Fable can run it mostly autonomously and hand back concrete deliverables, not just advice.

---

## The prompt

```
You are helping me launch FSK Codehouse Corp. — a Philippine-based software
studio — as if it were a startup entering the market for the first time, not
an established agency. We build three things: real-estate websites/listing
systems, digital product commerce storefronts, and web/mobile applications
(client work, partnerships, and our own products like Lenso, a private
photo-sharing app).

Do the following, in order, and give me real deliverables at the end —
not a summary of what I could do.

## 1. Philippines competitor research (public info only)

Research 8–12 Philippine web design/development studios and freelancers who
serve the same three service lines (real estate sites, ecommerce/digital
storefronts, web/mobile apps). Use only public sources: their own websites,
public Facebook Business Pages (read what's publicly posted — do not scrape,
export, or bulk-collect data, and do not join private groups or access
anything gated), Google, Clutch, GoodFirms, and LinkedIn company pages.

For each one, log: name, services offered, published pricing (if any),
positioning/tagline, and what makes them look different from us. Flag any
clear pricing or positioning gap we could exploit.

## 2. Global/foreign client acquisition research

Research how Philippine-based dev studios successfully win foreign clients
(US, UK, AU, EU) — this is the highest-leverage way to grow revenue, since
foreign clients pay in USD at rates well above the local PH market while our
delivery cost stays PH-based. Look into: Upwork/Toptal/Contra positioning,
Clutch/GoodFirms profiles, cold outbound on LinkedIn, SEO for
English-language search terms ("real estate website developer," "custom
ecommerce development agency," etc.), and referral/partnership channels.
Summarize which 2–3 channels are the best first bets for a studio our size,
with reasoning.

## 3. Pricing structure

Build a tiered pricing structure covering: real estate websites, digital
commerce storefronts, web & mobile apps, and supporting services (monthly
care/maintenance retainers, SEO, app store publishing, rush delivery). Set
TWO price bands per tier:
 - a PH-market band (competitive with what you found in step 1)
 - a global/foreign-client band (USD, benchmarked against international
   agency pricing, reflecting the PH cost advantage while staying credible
   for the quality bar we're claiming)
Show the reasoning for each band against the research from steps 1 and 2.

## 4. 90-day go-to-market plan

Write a concrete first-90-days plan: positioning statement, target client
profiles (PH SMBs/agents/brokerages AND foreign founders/SMEs), channel mix
and weekly cadence, first-client acquisition tactics (portfolio/case study
plan, cold outreach message templates, platform profile setup checklist for
Upwork/Clutch/LinkedIn), and 3–5 measurable milestones for day 30/60/90.

## Constraints

- No scraping, bots, bulk data export, or anything that violates a
  platform's terms of service. Public browsing and reading only.
- No fabricated competitor data — if you can't verify a number, say so
  instead of guessing.
- Give me finished tables/documents I can act on, not a checklist of what
  I should go research myself.
```

---

## Why it's framed this way

- The pricing dual-band approach (PH rate vs. global/USD rate) is the actual lever for "maximizing earnings" — it's standard practice for PH-based studios: same delivery cost, higher realized price from foreign clients.
- Facebook research is scoped to public browsing, not scraping — scraping/bulk export violates Meta's terms of service and risks Philippine Data Privacy Act exposure, so the prompt explicitly rules it out while still getting you the competitive intel.
- Step 4 forces a plan with actual outreach templates and channel picks, not generic advice.
