## PAGE 2 — Why this matters

*This page is sharper than the business version because the pain is specific and every agent feels it.*

```
WHY AGENTS LOSE LEADS

The buyer found the property.
They did not find you.

When your listings live only on Lamudi, OnePropertee, or Facebook
Marketplace, the enquiry goes to the platform. You are one of
several agents shown against the same property, and the buyer has
no reason to remember your name.

Your own site changes who owns the lead. The enquiry arrives in
your inbox, the buyer sees your credentials next to the property,
and nothing on the page is advertising a competitor.

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT COVERS

Four packages, from a single-page agent profile to a brokerage
system your staff can run themselves.
```
